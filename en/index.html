Our Philosophy

We believe furniture should outlast the trends that inspired it — so we build in steel, shaped to endure.

Material

201-grade stainless steel. Resistant to rust, stain, and time. It takes the light differently at every hour of the day.

Process

Each piece is cut, bent, welded, and hand-polished. The brushed finish is applied by hand. No two pieces are identical.

Form

Designed as sculpture first, furniture second. The negative space is as important as the steel itself.
