Returns & Cancellation Policy

Transaction Cancellation & Returns

A transaction may be cancelled within 14 days of receiving the product or receiving the transaction details, in accordance with the Consumer Protection Law.

The product must be returned unused, in its original condition.

Return shipping is at the customer's expense, unless the product was received damaged or differs from the order.

Custom & Made-to-Order Products

Products manufactured specifically for the customer — including special finishes or custom dimensions — cannot be cancelled or returned after production has begun, except in the case of a verified manufacturing defect.

Product Exchange

A product may be exchanged within 14 days of receipt, provided it has not been used and is returned in new, intact condition.

Inspection Upon Delivery

It is the customer's responsibility to inspect the product upon receipt and verify its condition. Claims regarding visible damage, dents, scratches, or defects will only be accepted at the time of delivery.

Manufacturing Defects & Warranty

Steelo provides a 12-month warranty on manufacturing defects only.

The warranty does not cover scratches, dents, signs of use, natural wear, or damage caused by improper use, cleaning, or maintenance.

Polishing marks, slight colour variations, fingerprints, and fine micro-scratches are natural characteristics of stainless steel and are not considered product defects.
